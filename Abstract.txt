Abstract
Nowadays, the Multi-Actor E-commerce System is a massive and sophisticated web application designed to revolutionize the online buying and selling process for businesses. This comprehensive platform provides a centralized hub for product postings, shopping cart management, order tracking, and dynamic profile dashboards. With a unique set of features, including seller management, product categorization, and role-based user authentication, the e-commerce system streamlines the process, reduces manual tasks, and improves overall market efficiency. Traditional retail systems often rely on manual processes such as physical inventory tracking and in-person transactions. Its main objective is to provide an efficient and well-structured trading workflow for administrators, sellers, and customers. This system allows customers to register, log in, browse available products, add items to their cart, securely place orders, and view their order history and status. Moreover, Sellers can register with their store details, manage their own product listings, and fulfill customer orders placed for their specific items. Furthermore, the Admin manages user registrations, can approve or reject new seller accounts, oversees product categories, and monitors all activities across the platform. The system is built following core functionalities and flexibility using modern web technologies to ensure system reliability, security, and scalability. By operating the digital retail process and reducing manual intervention, it accelerates transactions and improves efficiency. As a result, customers can save their time, avoid overcrowded physical stores, and streamline administrative tasks for the e-commerce platform.
